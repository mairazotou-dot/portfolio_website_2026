MERCHANDISE DESIGN

Initially designed for Dream Unit’s participation in the annual Order Tattoo Jam Festival in Amsterdam, the t-shirt was later incorporated into the store’s official merchandise collection. The lip tattoo design was inspired by my obsession with inner-lip tattoo placements.

The t-shirt prints were produced by hand using a Direct-to-Film heat-press method, in order for the vibrant colors, transparencies, and overall quality of the design to be preserved through everyday wear. I chose to print each shirt individually, giving me full control over the placement of each design.
