ABOUT THE CLIENT

Mora Momor is a fashion influencer, PR manager and stylist based between Amsterdam and Jakarta.

Inspired by her transformative approach to PR and styling, the visual identity of the project is based on the classic Cinderella fairytale, communicating her love for whimsy and nostalgic graphics.

For the creative direction I aimed to balance the playful, old-school elements, with soft pastel tones, in order to curate a cohesive aesthetic.

The logo designs have been used across her social media platforms, while the business cards have been printed and distributed.
