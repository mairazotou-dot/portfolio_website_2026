ABOUT SLEEZE

"SLEEZE - Dolls Kill" is an independent print magazine developed during my second year of Graphic Design studies.

Deeply inspired by feminism and the act of learning to accept yourself as a young woman, the publication became an outlet for what I think of as "female rage" - a refusal to neatly fit into the roles, aesthetics, and expectations placed on women.

Through unconventional fashion styling, personal essays, photography, and intentionally chaotic layouts, SLEEZE became more than an academic assignment. It gave me a space to push against the societal box and explore what exists outside of it.

The end result sits somewhere between a fashion publication and an unhinged conversation with your best friend and honestly- I could not have it any other way.
