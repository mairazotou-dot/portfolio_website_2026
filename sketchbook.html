ABOUT THE SKETCHBOOK

When starting a project, I often find myself overwhelmed by the thought of a blank canvas. Over time, I realised that the best way to overcome this fear was to put all my thoughts and ideas onto paper until there was barely any white space left.

My sketchbook has always been a space where I can create freely, with no grand expectations. Gradually, this way of working became an essential step to my creative process.

For a long time, I overlooked said process in favour of showcasing the final result. But, of course, there is no final result without all the steps that lead up to it.

Therefore, I couldn't think of a better way to end this portfolio than by sharing some of my favourite sketchbook spreads from 2025 and 2026.
