ABOUT ME

I am a graphic designer working across editorial design, web design, and creative coding, currently based between Rotterdam and Amsterdam.

My work is inspired by youth culture, fashion and their presence in the digital world-with all their spontaneity and creative mess.

If I could sum up my practice in one sentence, it would be:

“PLEASE BRING BACK FUN!”

I believe design should be allowed to be loud, bold and personal. The more fun you have making something, the more honest the result becomes.

Through publication design, web design, and fashion-oriented projects, I am aiming to translate that mindset through my visual language and design practice.

I want my work to feel alive. If it does, rest assured that I lived through it too. And if it makes you feel a little more alive too, then I have probably succeeded.
